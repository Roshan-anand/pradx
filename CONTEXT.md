# CONTEXT

## Glossary

- **Lead** — A person from a B2B prospect company who fills out the inquiry form with their name, email, and company details. They are not yet a customer.
- **Pradx** — The company that owns this landing page and receives Lead inquiry notifications.
- **Package** — A service tier a Lead expresses interest in. Options: Base, Pro, Super Pro. Selected from a dropdown on the inquiry form.
- **Lead Inquiry Form** — Collects: name, email, company name, company link (optional), interested package (optional).
