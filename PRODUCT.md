# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Creative clients and prospective partners browsing a brand/design agency's portfolio. They're evaluating capability, taste, and fit — the site itself is proof of craft. Their context is typically desktop browsing during a deliberate evaluation, not casual mobile scrolling.

## Product Purpose
Pradx Brand House is a creative agency portfolio that demonstrates brand design and digital craft capability. The site must attract and convert prospective clients by showcasing work with editorial confidence. Success is measured by inbound inquiries and perceived authority.

## Brand Personality
**Bold, premium, editorial.** The voice is confident and direct, never apologetic or tentative. The visual language draws from high-end editorial design — magazine-grade typography, deliberate whitespace, restrained color with dramatic moments. It feels like a design-forward publication, not a template.

## Anti-references
- SaaS-generic: white/cream backgrounds with a single blue accent, rounded cards everywhere, hero illustrations
- Startup-template: gradient text, glass cards, hero-metric grids, tiny uppercase eyebrows above every section
- Corporate-conservative: navy-and-beige palettes, safe sans-serif defaults, no typographic personality
- AI-default "warm" tones: cream/sand/beige body backgrounds at OKLCH chroma 0.005-0.02 toward yellow

## Design Principles
1. **Confidence over caution** — Visual choices commit. No hedging with safe defaults or template patterns.
2. **Editorial-first** — Typography, rhythm, and whitespace do the heavy lifting. Layouts feel like a magazine page, not a dashboard.
3. **Restraint earns drama** — Mostly monochrome with one deliberate color accent. When color appears, it feels intentional.
4. **Craft is the content** — The site itself is the portfolio. Every detail (typography, motion, spacing, hover states) proves capability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA minimum. All body text ≥4.5:1 contrast against background. Large text ≥3:1. Keyboard-navigable, screen-reader-accessible. Reduced-motion fallbacks for all animations.
